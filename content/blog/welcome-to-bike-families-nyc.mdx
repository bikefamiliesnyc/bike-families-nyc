We're thrilled to welcome you to **Bike Families NYC**, a community dedicated to making cycling with kids safer, more accessible, and more fun across all five boroughs.

## Our Mission

New York City has made incredible strides in building bike infrastructure over the past decade. Protected bike lanes, Citi Bike, and car-free streets have transformed how many New Yorkers get around. But for families with young children, biking can still feel intimidating.

That's where we come in.

## What We Do

### Family Rides

Every month, we organize group rides designed specifically for families. Our routes avoid busy streets, include plenty of rest stops, and move at a pace that works for everyone—whether your kiddo is on their own bike, in a trailer, or riding on a cargo bike with you.

### Cargo Bike Library

Curious about cargo bikes but not ready to invest? Our lending library lets you try different models to see what works for your family before you buy.

### Bike Bus

We help organize Bike Buses—groups of kids and families who ride to school together along set routes. There's safety in numbers, and kids love biking with their friends!

## Join Us

Whether you're a seasoned cycling family or just bike-curious, we'd love to have you. Check out our [upcoming events](/events) or just show up to a ride. Everyone is welcome.

See you on the streets!
